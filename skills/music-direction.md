# Music direction

Decide the sound plan during proposal/direction, not after picture lock.

1. State the narrative job of music: momentum, tension, warmth, punctuation, continuity, or intentional silence.
2. Inspect available project audio and `media.search-stock` candidates. Record source page, creator and license before acquisition.
3. Offer explicit choices: selected project track, rights-checked stock, externally generated/provided track, or no music.
4. Define entrance, exit, loop policy, target level, fade and ducking against speech. Avoid using one uninterrupted energy level for the whole film.
5. After render, inspect loudness/clipping and listen in full. Technical levels do not prove that the track supports the story.
