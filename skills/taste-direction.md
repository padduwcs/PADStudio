# Taste direction

Turn references into a small, testable visual language before producing many assets.

- Name the intended audience response, hierarchy, information density, palette, typography, texture, motion intensity and pacing.
- Separate qualities worth preserving from literal elements that should not be copied.
- Define three to five anti-patterns, such as generic gradients, repeated centered cards, constant zoom, stock imagery with mismatched lighting, or motion without narrative purpose.
- Plan meaningful variation across shots: scale, composition, depth, camera energy, text density and visual source may change while the core language remains coherent.
- Bind selected references and rationale to the creative direction artifact. A style playbook is optional and must be selected explicitly.

Review actual rendered frames at multiple times; do not approve taste from prompts or metadata alone.
