# Result review

## Use when

A result or artifact must be checked before continuation, delivery, or approval.

## Method

1. Read the target, its purpose, relevant brief/direction, and the producing run.
2. Evaluate technical integrity and creative effectiveness separately.
3. For each criterion, record evidence and a status.
4. Every failure must include a concrete proposed action.
5. Record a verdict; do not silently repair or approve on the user's behalf.

## Standard

Passing technical checks does not prove creative success. Subjective feedback must still be tied to stated intent and observable evidence.
